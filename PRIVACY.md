# Privacy Policy for PowerAudio

**Last updated:** December 23, 2024

## Overview

PowerAudio is a desktop audio visualization application. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection

**PowerAudio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

### Audio Data
- PowerAudio captures your system's audio output solely for the purpose of generating real-time visualizations
- Audio data is processed locally on your device in real-time
- Audio data is never recorded, stored, or transmitted outside your device
- Audio processing stops immediately when the application is closed

### No Analytics or Tracking
- PowerAudio does not include any analytics or tracking software
- No usage data is collected
- No crash reports are sent
- No data is transmitted over the internet

### No Account Required
- PowerAudio does not require user registration
- No personal information is requested or stored

## Third-Party Services

PowerAudio does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data.

## Data Storage

PowerAudio does not store any data on your device beyond its installation files.

## Children's Privacy

PowerAudio does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
